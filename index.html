Artifact 2

Focusqueue

You add each task with a title, a short description, a due date, and a difficulty of low, medium, or high. A group name is optional, so a task can belong to a class, a project, a client, or to no group. You can view one group or every task at once. Either way, the easiest tasks with the closest due dates come first.
